## Design Context

### Users
Energy industry professionals, corporate clients (insurance companies, utility companies, engineering firms), and engineers seeking specialized training in renewable energy and risk engineering. Users are typically browsing in professional settings during work hours, looking for credible training programs and risk engineering services.

### Brand Personality
Professional, authoritative, and technically competent. The voice should convey expertise, trustworthiness, and industry leadership in renewable energy training and risk engineering. Emotional goals: confidence, credibility, and professional growth.

### Aesthetic Direction
Clean & minimal visual style with light mode as primary. Emphasis on strong typography, spacious layouts, and subtle refinements. The design should feel authoritative without being overwhelming, with careful attention to hierarchy and readability. Reference: professional consulting firms, technical training institutions, and energy sector leaders.

### Design Principles
1. **Clarity over decoration** - Every element should serve a purpose; avoid visual noise
2. **Professional hierarchy** - Strong typographic scale with clear content structure
3. **Trust through restraint** - Confident use of white space and minimal color accents
4. **Technical credibility** - Clean, precise layouts that reflect engineering excellence
5. **Accessibility first** - High contrast, readable typography, and clear navigation
